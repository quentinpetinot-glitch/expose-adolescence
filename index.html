3. The story : what happens ?

No difficult words. I explain simply.

Jamie Miller is 13 years old. He lives in England with his mum, his dad and his sister.

One morning, the police come to his house. They arrest Jamie. He is accused of killing Katie, a girl from his school.

Jamie says : "I did nothing." His father is shocked. He cries.

The police work. They go to the school. They see bad messages on Instagram. Some boys insult girls. It is cyberbullying.

A psychologist talks with Jamie. It is a long talk, 52 minutes. Jamie is angry, then sad, then strange.

At the end, we see the family one year later. Life is hard. The father cries in the room of his son.

Owen Cooper = Jamie

He is 15 years old. First role. Very good.

Stephen Graham = Eddie

He is the father. He is sad.

Erin Doherty = Briony

She is the psychologist.
