3. The story : what happens ?

The full story, episode by episode. Simple words.

Jamie Miller is 13 years old. He lives in England with his mum Manda, his dad Eddie and his big sister Lisa. It is a normal family. Eddie works as a plumber. Life is quiet.

Episode 1 — The arrest

One morning at 6 o'clock, the police break the door of the house. It is violent and fast. They arrest Jamie. He is accused of killing Katie, a girl from his school. Katie was found dead with a knife.

Jamie cries and says : "I did nothing, it wasn't me." His father Eddie is shocked. He goes with Jamie to the police station because Jamie is a child. At the station, the police take photos, fingerprints and clothes. Jamie is afraid. His father does not understand. The viewer does not understand too.

Episode 2 — The school

Two inspectors, Bascombe and Frank, go to Jamie's school. They want answers. They talk to pupils and teachers. The school is noisy. Nobody respects the teachers.

Bascombe's son shows him the truth on Instagram. Pupils use emojis with secret meanings. A heart or a dynamite emoji is an insult. Katie and her friends laughed at Jamie. They called him "incel", a boy that girls reject. Jamie was angry and sad. The inspectors understand : social media is a violent world. Parents do not see it.

Episode 3 — The psychologist

Briony is a psychologist. She meets Jamie in prison. They talk for 52 minutes, alone in a room. At first Jamie is polite. He eats, he jokes. Then Briony asks about Katie. Jamie becomes angry. He shouts. He scares her.

Jamie says Katie was mean with him. He felt small. He wanted respect. This episode is the most famous. There are only two actors in one room, but it is very scary.

Episode 4 — The family, one year later

Jamie is in prison. We do not see him. We see his family 13 months after the murder. It is Eddie's birthday. The family wants a normal day, but the van of Eddie is painted with the word "nonce". Neighbours hate them.

At the supermarket, Eddie fights with a boy. At home, the family cries. Eddie goes to Jamie's room. He puts the teddy bear on the bed. He says "sorry" and cries. The series ends like this. It is very sad.

Owen Cooper = Jamie

He is 15 years old. First role. Very good.

Stephen Graham = Eddie

He is the father. He is sad.

Erin Doherty = Briony

She is the psychologist.

4. Why I like Adolescence

My opinion. Simple English.

  • I like the story. It is strong. I think about my phone and school.
  • I like the actors. Owen Cooper is young but he plays very well. The father cries for real, I think.
  • I like the camera. One episode = one shot, no cut. It is like real life. I feel stress.
  • I like episode 3. Only two people talk in a room. It is simple but scary.
  • I learn a lesson. Words on the internet can hurt. Parents must talk with children.

Conclusion : I love this series. It is short, 4 episodes. I recommend it to my class. Thank you for listening!
